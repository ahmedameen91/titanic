Patterns the classifier can learn from passenger attributes.

ⓘ  These are associations in a historical dataset, not causes or guarantees for an individual passenger.

How this model works

01 / 03
01   Prepare features

Passenger class, sex, age, fare, family counts, and embarkation port. Missing values are imputed from the training data.

02   Fit logistic regression

A binary classifier learns feature weights and returns a survival probability.

03   Evaluate fairly

Stratified 80/20 train-validation split. The final model is then refit on all labeled rows.
